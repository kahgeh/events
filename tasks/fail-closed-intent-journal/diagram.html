Fail Closed with Pending Appends

The current protocol can recover after a crash, but it still has a live-service gap: events may be durable while the catalog head is stale and the store keeps accepting writes. The target protocol records intent explicitly and refuses more appends until recovery resolves it.

Current Recoverable Path

  • No durable flag says the store is temporarily unsafe to write.
  • Recovery infers incomplete work indirectly from partition contents.

Target Intent-Journal Path

  • Recovery is precise, auditable, and bounded to known in-flight appends.
  • Writers fail closed instead of continuing on stale metadata.
